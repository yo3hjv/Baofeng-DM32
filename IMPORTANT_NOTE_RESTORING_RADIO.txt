A short note about restoring the FW. 
After extensive testing, I found that the FLASH memory where the FW is stored has an inconsistency in writing to one of the addresses. 
The content of that address could be erased but could not be written so that DM32 did not work. 
After replacing it with another chip, it was necessary to rewrite the GD25Q80 flash with a bin that I obtained from another station. 
Unfortunately, no matter what combinations I tried, the station displays the error "Incorrect HW version" in Chinese. 
The conclusion is that restoring with the Flash memory image obtained from another station is impossible, as a specific hardware encoding is involved. 
I did the reading/writing of the GD25Q80 flash with an external reader and NeoProgrammer. 

From this point on, it becomes more convenient to purchase a new station and throw the defective one in the bin.
